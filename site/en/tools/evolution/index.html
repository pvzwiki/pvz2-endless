How to follow a setup
  1. Fully quit and relaunch the game.
  2. Complete each listed preview, including its placement effects, at the stated Sunflower cost, then enter the level directly. The calculation includes what the level shuffles as it loads.
  3. Use the listed plants, costs, cells and planting order.
  4. Start the waves and activate promptly at the chosen position. Read the results before other events change the board.
