Cultivation

All artifacts share one table: a level upgrade costs vials and needs the rank shown; a rank upgrade needs the level shown, cubes and gold.

Level upgrade
Levels 1–9: 10 vials · needs rank 1Levels 10–19: 20 vials · needs rank 2Levels 20–29: 30 vials · needs rank 3
Rank 1 → 2
at level 10: 10 cubes · 50,000 gold
Rank 2 → 3
at level 20: 30 cubes · 100,000 gold
Rank 3 → 4
at level 30: 50 cubes · 200,000 gold
